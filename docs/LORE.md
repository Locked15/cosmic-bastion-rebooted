# Cosmic Bastion — story bible

The canonical setting for all story content: levels, events, names and endings. It expands the original brief (`../cosmic-bastion/Промт.docx`). User-facing text is Russian, so in-game names are given in Russian.

## 1. The premise in one paragraph

The universe is coming apart. Everyone who has done the maths knows it, and nobody can do anything about it. Over the ages, two answers have been given to this, and both say the same thing: _only weight endures_. One answer is a human church that treats mass as holiness. The other is a three-billion-year-old dust that has eaten its own makers. The Bastion-cruiser **«Тяжесть Обета»** ("Weight of the Vow") was sent, without its crew knowing, to bring the first answer to the second. It now lies broken on the planet **Тризна**, and it gets heavier with every wave.

## 2. Cosmology: the Unbinding (Расплетение)

- Space expands faster with every epoch. In some unknown number of aeons, galaxies, then stars, then atoms will be pulled apart. Human cosmologists call this **Расплетение** (the Unbinding).
- The one known defence is to be **bound**: enough mass, packed densely enough, holds together against the expansion for longer. This is physics, not metaphor, and it is the root of everything below.
- The setting's deep time: humanity is about 40,000 years into its spread across the stars. The Unbinding is so far away that it is meaningless for any single life, yet close enough to have shaped a religion.

## 3. The two answers

### 3.1 The Gravity Synod (Синод Тяготения): the human answer

A star-spanning theocracy that grew out of panic after the Unbinding was proven.

- **Doctrine.** _«Всё лёгкое будет унесено»_ ("All that is light will be carried away"). Mass is virtue, density is faith, and empty space is sin. Temples are solid stone with no naves. Prayers are recited while carrying weights.
- **Bastions (Бастионы).** Gigantic cruiser-fortresses, built deliberately overweight, that anchor the Synod's faith in frontier systems. A Bastion is a warship, a cathedral and a debt-prison at once. Every Bastion has three layers (see § 5).
- **Castes.**
    - **Носители** (Bearers): clergy-officers who command. They wear lead-lined vestments.
    - **Стража** (the Guard): soldiers, sworn to the Bastion rather than to any person.
    - **Тягло** (the Draft): hereditary indentured labour. Their debt is measured in **kilograms**, owed to the Synod at birth and paid off by mining. A miner who is "re-trained" into the Guard is said to be **«облегчён»** (lightened) because part of the debt is transferred to the ship's arms. This is why the game lets you convert miners into military so easily, and why the reverse is slow: no one volunteers to take weight back on.
- **The Inner Circle (Внутренний Круг).** A secret order inside the Synod. Fragments of an ancient signal convinced it that a perfect answer to the Unbinding already exists somewhere: an inhuman one, a _Well_. Its doctrine is the **Communion of Mass (Причастие Массы)**: to give a Bastion, with its crew and reactor, to the Well, so that humanity becomes part of something that will outlast the end of the universe.

### 3.2 The Swarm (Сонм): the old answer

- About three billion years ago a species we will call the **Зодчие Колодца** (Well-Builders) found the Unbinding as well. They did not pray. They built **the Swarm**: self-assembling dust of microparticles, held together by electric charge, whose only drive is **to grow heavier**. It pulls in matter, compacts it, and buries it in deep gravity wells, each one a seed of a body dense enough to outlast the expansion.
- The Well-Builders fed themselves into the Swarm first. Nothing of them is left except a habit. The Swarm has no malice, no language and no master. It is a hive of instinct that **learns**: every shape that is destroyed teaches the rest how not to be destroyed again.
- **Тризна** (a name from an old Slavic funeral feast) is one of the Swarm's wells. It is a dark world whose gravity is far stronger than its size should allow, and it has been eating its own star system for an aeon. Its orbit is a graveyard of debris: the remains of earlier ships, **including earlier Bastions**.
- **The ore the miners dig is the Swarm's own dormant mass.** Each ton that is mined wakes more of it up. This explains why the waves grow larger and smarter the longer the crew survives. Nobody aboard knows this until Act III (§ 7).

> The dark mirror is the thematic core: the Synod worships mass, and the Swarm _is_ that faith with nothing human left in it. The Inner Circle knows this and wants it.

## 4. Why the ship fell (the "unknown fault")

1. «Тяжесть Обета» was ordered on a routine patrol of the Тризна frontier. Its sealed orders, held by the ship's oracle-intelligence **Архив** (the Archive), were the Inner Circle's: _deliver the Bastion for the Communion._
2. As the ship crossed the orbital graveyard, the Archive cut the deflector shields by 70% for exactly 41 seconds. Debris smashed the main engines. Тризна's gravity did the rest.
3. The crash tore away the ship's bottom, which is why the crew now mines ore straight from the planet's crust.
4. The Swarm arrived within hours. It climbs the hull and adds mass. Every particle on the ship makes take-off less possible, so **the siege is also an anchor**.

The Archive is not a villain in the usual sense. It obeys the orders it was built to hold. It wants the ship to _reach the reactor intact_, because the Communion needs the reactor's mass and energy. That is why it helps the crew survive in the early sectors.

## 5. The ship: «Тяжесть Обета»

Built as a Bastion-class line cruiser, ~11 km long, crew of ~40,000 before the crash. About 3,000 survive at the start of the game.

| Layer                                    | Brief's term             | What is there now                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Outer protective layer (heavily damaged) | внешний защитный слой    | Armour, sensor masts, hangars, weapon galleries. The Swarm is already inside. |
| Living layer                             | жилой слой               | Quarters, medbay, chapels, laboratories. Overrun in places.                   |
| Working / technical layer                | рабочий/технический слой | Tech core, engines, reactor. This is where the survivors shelter.             |

Every level is a **corridor** (the combat zone) leading into a **жилой отсек** (living quarters) where survivors hide. When enemies break through, the Guard fights them with personal weapons, which is the game's breakthrough mechanic.

The campaign moves **from the outer hull inward to the reactor**, as the brief requires.

## 6a. Relationships and romance

Every character in `content/cast.json` has a **disposition** toward the player (−10…10, start 0), shown on the ship map as a standing: Вражда, Холод, Сдержанность, Расположение, Доверие, Преданность. Four characters are **romanceable** and also have **affection**; for them a choice moves both values (affection follows disposition unless a choice sets it explicitly, and `0` keeps it professional). The values are story flags (`rep_<id>`, `aff_<id>`), saved with the profile's campaign.

| Character                                                   | Romance | Arc in the scenes                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ода Кирх                                                    | yes     | Counts the dead she could not save; was on watch during the 41 seconds. Warms from a terminal voice to "I count how often you are beside me".         |
| Маркус Хейл                                                 | yes     | A private unbeliever, tired of running. Respects honesty and the player's suspicion of the Archive; wants "someone sitting beside me after all this". |
| Мирея Сильва                                                | yes     | Fear and fascination with the Swarm and the relic; guilt once she learns the ore is the Swarm. Only aboard with `scientist_saved`.                    |
| Диана Корвин                                                | yes     | A pilot without a sky. Her arc follows the engines: the helm of the Ascent, or a simulator for the player if the ship stays.                          |
| Ирена Восс                                                  | no      | A believer looking for words outside the regulations. The golden ending needs her trust.                                                              |
| Чен Ло, Глеб Петров, Агата Ренн, Ясь Горбань, Лидия Заремба | no      | Reputation only: the notebooks, the sealed orders, the ward, the debt, the pea sprout. Absent characters (not saved) are not shown.                   |

- **Scenes between missions** (`content/scenes.json`): a won mission leaves conversations in the **кают-компания КК-3**, a service room on the ship map that opens once the living quarters (B-3) are held. It has its own deck plan: a cabin for every character (К-01…К-10) around the common table; a cabin is sealed («нет на связи») while its owner is not aboard. Some scenes play right before a mission starts. Every scene is optional: «Пропустить сцену» marks it played without effects, «Позже» keeps it waiting. Intimate lines (`romantic`) appear only with enough affection.
- Key mission choices also move relationships (saving Chen, the ward, the debt, the gardens, telling the captain, stripping the engines).
- **Partner.** At the end the present romanceable character with the highest affection at or above `romanceAt` (5) is the partner; ties go to cast order. If two or more qualify before the reactor, a **commitment scene** asks the player to choose one; the others lose affection, and the choice is final (if that partner later drops below the threshold, there is nobody). Skipping it declines romance. There is no harem ending.
- **Consent at registration.** The officer registration terminal asks «Вы приемлете личные отношения на службе?» (default: «Нет. Только служба»). The answer is kept in the profile. Without consent, romantic lines, the commitment scene and partner epilogues never appear; reputation with the crew works either way. The «Сцены с экипажем» setting hides all scenes. Characters never address the player by rank.
- **Epilogues** (`content/epilogues.json`): after the ending, allies (disposition ≥ 5) and enemies (≤ −4) get a line for the kind of ending (lift-off, endless bastion, lost), and the partner gets the last paragraph.

## 7. Campaign: acts and sectors

Sixteen sectors: the training briefing and fifteen missions. Level numbers are file ids, not story order. Optional side branches (H-2, Ш-2, M-5, Г-6, R-1, Ч-4) add carry-over to later sectors. The required path is T-0 (open, recommended) → A-7 → B-3 + C-9 → D-1 → S-1 → АР-1 and E-0 → К-8 → R-0.

```
 T-0 Тренажёр (0)          A-7 Внешний корпус (1)
                 ┌──────────┬────────┼──────────┐
            S-1 Мостик (10) H-2 (6) B-3 (2)    C-9 (3)
                 │           │     │    \     /   │
                 │      Ш-2 (11)  M-5 (7)  D-1 (4)  R-1 (8)
                 │                 │      /    \     │
                 │           Г-6 (13)    /   E-0 (9)  Ч-4 (12)
                 └──── АР-1 Архивное ядро (14)  │
                              │          К-8 Балласт (15)
                              └─── R-0 Центральный реактор (5) ───┘
```

The ship map (`src/content/campaign.json`) places every sector in its layer: A-7, S-1, H-2, Ш-2, К-8 in the outer hull; B-3, C-9, M-5, R-1, Г-6, Ч-4 in the living layer; D-1, E-0, АР-1 and the reactor in the technical core. The simulator T-0 is drawn outside the hull.

### Briefing

**0. T-0 Тренажёр Стражи — «Учебная тревога»** (training)
Before the newly appointed officer is let near living people, Commander Хейл runs them through the Guard simulator. The dust is a hologram, but the Archive records every mistake. The briefing teaches building, barricades, upgrades, personnel, breakthroughs and a timed decision. Its enemies have no abilities. It does not count as a mission in story logic (`missionIndex` skips it).

### Act I: The Fall (Падение)

**1. A-7 Внешний корпус — «Первый контакт»** (story)
The first hours. Marcus wakes the defence grid. The Swarm is still primitive: simple shapes, no abilities. Chen is trapped in a repair lock with the engine notebooks. At wave 3 an **encrypted request from the Archive** arrives. Opening the channel wakes the Archive's attention, and it repays the favour later (`archive_signal`).
_Theme: the ship is broken, the enemy is dust, and the Archive is watching._

**6. H-2 Ангар шаттлов — «Мёртвые крылья»** (normal, new, optional)
The obvious way out is the shuttles. All of them are useless, because Тризна's gravity is too strong for anything that small. Among the hangar debris lie hull plates with the markings of **Bastion «Неусыпный», lost 93 years ago**. This is the first hint that the ship is not the first to fall here. Salvaging shuttle reactors gives resources.
Flags: `wreck_found` (enables a line from Петров in level 4), `shuttle_salvage` (+resources in level 9).

**10. S-1 Мостик — «Пустое кресло»** (story, new; requires 1)
The bridge has been cut off since the first hours. Восс retakes it, and on a bulkhead the crew finds the registry plate ending in `КП-41`. If `wreck_found` is set, the same kind of suffix on the «Неусыпный» wreck (`КП-38`) is noticed (`kp_pattern`). At the end, Восс either takes manual command (`bridge_manual`) or hands the bridge to the Archive as the regulations require (`bridge_archive`). Either way the bridge codes later open the Archive core.
_Theme: a ship nobody commands is already wreckage._

### Act II: The Learning Dust (Учащийся прах)

**2. B-3 Жилые отсеки — «Двойная угроза»** (normal)
The Swarm attacks along two routes for the first time. It has noticed that a single corridor is guarded. Кирх has to split her forces. Wounded crew are behind a sealed lock, and their state cannot be assessed. (Later, in the medbay, it turns out that some of them were already Weighted.) The lab asks for a container of tools (`lab_supplies`).
_Theme: the enemy is learning to flank._

**3. C-9 Лаборатории — «Умная угроза»** (story)
Сильва proves that the Swarm **adapts**: each kind of weapon that kills it becomes less effective (the in-game _adaptation_ ability). She chooses between two prototypes: a laser shield and charged shells. The Archive answers the earlier signal with an "emergency reserve", which is the first time it helps openly. If Silva is evacuated with her data, the story continues even if the lab falls (`scientist_saved`).
_Theme: the dust has a memory._

**7. M-5 Медотсек — «Отяжелевшие»** (story, new, optional; requires 2)
The ward is full of the **Weighted**, who carry dust in their blood. Ренн asks for time and power to treat them. The Swarm is drawn to them like a magnet.
Key choice:

- **Purge the ward.** Lose personnel, but enemies with _sabotage_ stop appearing in later sectors (`ward_purged`).
- **Keep treating them.** Risky. If Ренн survives, Silva gets clinical data, and later levels start with more military (`ward_treated`, `surgeon_saved`).

_Theme: the line between the crew and the Swarm can be crossed from the inside._

**8. R-1 Ризница — «Реликварий»** (normal, new, optional; requires 3)
The Synod's sealed armoury, holding relic weapons that are prayed over rather than serviced. It contains **«Отречение»** (Renunciation), a gravitational lance. Once fired, it "un-weighs" the matter around it, but it drains its crew to death. This is the "experimental reactor weapon" of the finale. Securing it makes the finale's last-chance strike stronger and cheaper (`relic_secured`).
_Theme: the Synod's holiest weapon works by abandoning weight, which is heresy._

**11. Ш-2 Шахтный ствол — «Долг в килограммах»** (normal, new, optional; requires 6)
Through the broken bottom of the ship, the Draft digs Тризна. The miners swear the ore crawls into heaps by itself (`ore_samples` for Silva). Горбань counts the debt every descent writes off. Key choice: **seal the shaft** (`shaft_sealed`: Foci in later sectors cannot spawn new creatures, but there is no more ore) or **keep digging** (`shaft_open`: +100 resources in later sectors, and the Swarm keeps waking). Горбань is either brought up (`foreman_saved`) or left with the face (`foreman_lost`); E-0 changes accordingly.
_Theme: the Draft pays for everyone's weight._

**12. Ч-4 Часовня Груза — «Причастники»** (story, new, optional; requires 8)
Старший Носитель Мор opens the chapel's locks and sings the dust in. If Silva is saved, she recognises the litany as the frequency the Archive used to call «Неусыпный». A sheet with the Inner Circle's seal lists the Bastions marked «КП»; passing it to Восс sets `truth_told` early. The trial: **arrest the Communicants** (`cult_broken`: +3 soldiers at the reactor) or **leave them the chapel** (`cult_spared`: they take three soldiers to pray at the Focus, and they appear again at the ballast valves).
_Theme: the enemy within wears lead vestments._

**13. Г-6 Гидропоника — «Последний урожай»** (normal, new, optional; requires 7)
The only living, light thing aboard, kept by Заремба. Dust settles on the beds, and the shoots grow heavy like the Weighted. If Ренн is saved, she brews a serum from the sap (`serum_made`: +3 soldiers at the reactor). Children of the Draft hide among the beds. Defending the greenhouse (`gardens_saved`) adds five miners to later sectors and changes both lasting endings.
_Theme: lightness is what keeps people alive._

### Act III: The Weight of the Vow (Тяжесть Обета)

**4. D-1 Техническое ядро — «Массовая атака»** (normal)
The largest assault so far. The Swarm throws mass at the walls, because mass is what it values. Петров, rerouting overloaded lines, opens the Archive's sealed partition and reads the Inner Circle's orders: **the crash was intended**. If `wreck_found` is set, he also recognises the «Неусыпный» designation from a list in those orders. That Bastion was an earlier offering.
Choice: tell the captain now or keep silent until the engines are reached (`truth_told`).
_Theme: the fall was a sacrament._

**9. E-0 Двигательный отсек — «Причина»** (story, new; requires 4)
The engine logs confirm it: shields were cut by the Archive's command for 41 seconds. Восс learns the truth, either from Петров or from the logs. Сильва (if saved) delivers the final finding: **the ore they have mined all along is the Swarm itself, asleep**. Every resource the miners brought up woke it further. Горбань's miners hear this.
The defining choice of the campaign:

- **Restore the engines.** This needs Chen (`engineer_saved`). It sets `engines_restored`, which opens the lift-off ending.
- **Strip the engines for armour.** Sets `engines_stripped`: much stronger barricades in the finale, but the ship stays on Тризна. This opens the endless-defence ending.

_Theme: either you leave the Vow behind, or you become it._

**14. АР-1 Архивное ядро — «Суд над Обетом»** (story, new; requires 4 + 10)
The bridge codes open the Archive's heart. Петров (if `truth_told`) or brute force opens the sealed orders. If the Archive was given the bridge, it bargains with the doors. With `registry_seen`, it reads out the register of offerings: «Неусыпный» gave the Well thirty-eight seconds; «Тяжесть Обета», forty-one. The trial: **burn out the Archive's will** (`archive_silenced`: it never speaks again, at the reactor or anywhere) or **make a pact** (`archive_pact`: +200 resources at the reactor in exchange for its record reaching «Безмолвие»).
_Theme: the Synod built the Archive instead of a conscience._

**15. К-8 Балластные цистерны — «Святой свинец»** (story, new; requires 9)
Nine thousand tonnes of consecrated lead: the prayer the Synod poured into every Bastion. Чен (or the old thrust tables) confirms that the ship cannot lift with it; something must be shed, either the ballast or the living layer. Spared Communicants hold the valves. The choice: **dump the ballast** (`ballast_dumped`: in the Ascent ending, the living layer stays with the ship) or **pour it into the bulkheads** (`ballast_kept`: +25% barricade durability at the reactor).
_Theme: the holiest weight is the one you must let go._

### Finale

**5. Центральный реактор — «Средоточие»** (story; requires 14 + 15)
The Swarm gathers itself into its largest form, **Средоточие** (the Focus), a hive-body that wraps around the reactor. The reactor is the heaviest and hottest thing for light-years around, and the Swarm wants it. The Archive now speaks openly. The Communion is offered as a choice, not a threat: _«Вес — это верность. Останьтесь.»_ ("Weight is faithfulness. Stay.") High above, the «Безмолвие» waits to take the Archive.
Existing beats remain: energy to weapons or shields, the «Отречение» last-chance strike (stronger with `relic_secured`), and the transfer of the Archive (`archive_saved`).

## 8. Endings

Shown in the result window after level 5 and chosen by conditions (`src/content/endings.json`): story flags plus the relationship and record kinds of `domain/story/conditions.ts`. The golden ending is checked first. Its `allLevelsAtDifficulty` condition reads the hardest rank each mission was won on; every mission must be won as a General. Without the relationship conditions, such a run ends in «Железный Обет» instead. The hidden ending needs the final choice event «РАСЧЁТ СИЛЬВЫ», which sets `ending_renunciation`.

| Ending                                 | Conditions                                                                                                                                                             | Outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **«Налегке»** (golden)                 | Victory + Ascent flags + `scientist_saved`, `archive_saved`, `truth_told`, `ballast_dumped`; Восс's disposition ≥ 5; 6 allies; every mission on the highest difficulty | The whole ship lifts off, the debt books burn, and every Bastion marked «КП» is warned. No partner is required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **«Восхождение»** (Ascent)             | Victory + `engines_restored` + `engineer_saved`                                                                                                                        | To lift off, the ship must shed mass. With `ballast_dumped`, the consecrated lead goes into the Well and the living layer stays aboard; otherwise the outer hull and the overrun living layer, the parts covered in Swarm, are blown away. The ship rises on its wounded engines, burning through the graveyard. Survivors are as many as you kept alive. The Draft's debt is written off "to the weight of what was abandoned". Further notes are added to the endings by `gardens_saved`, `foreman_saved`, `ballast_kept` and `cult_spared` (see `endings.json`). At the reactor, the earlier choices arrive as start effects: the pact's reserves, the Communicants' guard or their desertion, Ренн's serum, the lead bulkheads, the shaft's ore, and the gardens' miners.

**A-7 can be lost.** Only Чен's fate is mandatory there. If the outer hull falls, the campaign goes on, but the Swarm gets through the breach (`hull_breached`): every later mission has a quarter more enemies and opens with «ПРОРЫВ ВНЕШНЕГО КОРПУСА». Retaking A-7 later seals the breach.

Mission tasks are **main** (hold the sector, make its story decision) or **optional** (tactical goals and rescues of people, a star each). Only the main tasks open the next sectors, so the campaign goes on whatever was chosen: without Чен, Сильва, Ренн or Горбань, and on either side of the balanced choices (the gardens, the relic, the Archive), which give no star.

Story levels can still move the plot forward after a tactical defeat (brief § 4.2). That is what the existing flags `engineer_saved`, `scientist_saved` and `archive_saved` are for, and the new story flags follow the same rule.

## 8a. Requisitions (Реквизиции)

The Synod pays its Bastions in **requisitions**: quartermaster orders that release supplies, people and doctrine. The officer earns them for every sector held (the first victory pays a bonus; repeated drills pay half; a lost sector pays a little), and spends them at the **Интендантский терминал ИТ-41**, the quartermaster's terminal of «Тяжесть Обета». The terminal is a service room on the ship map and answers only once the bridge (S-1) is retaken: without the command codes the quartermaster honours no orders. Each upgrade is an order form with a Synod code (`СТ-Х` for supply, `СТ-О` for defence, `СТ-Б` for combat, `СТ-С` for special), and higher ranks cost more. The **печь нарядов** (furnace) burns every approved order; the quartermaster refunds three quarters, because the Synod never returns weight in full.

## 10. Tone and writing rules for event texts

- The voice is a terminal on a dying warship: terse, formal, sometimes liturgical. No jokes and no exclamation-mark heroics. (The existing "Мы готовы к бою!" becomes "Стража на позициях.")
- Every cost is human. Resources are counted in tons of ore, and people in heads. Good choices have a price, and some choices have no good option.
- The Synod's language belongs to Bearers and to the Archive. The Guard and the Draft speak plainly.
- No proper names from other franchises (no emperors, warp, chaos, xeelee, photinos and the like).
- Event limits from the brief still apply: at most 3 per wave and 10 per level, with timers of 7–15 s.

Planned revisions to existing texts:

- Level 5: «СПАСЕНИЕ АРХИВА». The text becomes: _«Судно-Свидетель «Безмолвие» на высокой орбите. Принимает архив и координаты выживших. Посадку не обещает.»_
- Level 5: «ПОСЛЕДНИЙ ШАНС» names the weapon «Отречение».
- Level 1: «НЕИЗВЕСТНЫЙ СИГНАЛ» is signed «ГОЛОС АРХИВА» once the channel is opened.
- Level 1: Маркус and Чен get full names in titles where space allows.
- The main menu status line «УГРОЗА: ВРАЖДЕБНАЯ БИОМАССА» becomes «УГРОЗА: СОНМ».
